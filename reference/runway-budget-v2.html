          🎓 Mode are you in school, or paying it off?

          Unsubsidized and private loans rack up interest while you're enrolled. If you don't touch it, that interest capitalizes — it's bolted onto your principal when repayment starts, and then you pay interest on the interest. Throwing even a little at it now stops that.
